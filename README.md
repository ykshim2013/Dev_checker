A web-based tool to track and lookup developmental milestones for children from birth to 6 years old.

## Features

- **Easy Age Input**: Enter age in months or years
- **Comprehensive Milestones**: Covers developmental milestones from 2 months to 6 years
- **Organized Categories**: Milestones grouped by:
  - Social & Emotional Development
  - Language & Communication
  - Cognitive Development
  - Motor Skills
- **Responsive Design**: Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices
- **User-Friendly Interface**: Clean, modern design with gradient styling

## How to Use

1. Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. Enter the child's age in the input field
3. Select the unit (months or years)
4. Click "Check Milestones" or press Enter
5. View the developmental milestones for that age range

## Age Ranges Covered

- 2 months
- 4 months
- 6 months
- 9 months
- 12 months (1 year)
- 18 months
- 2 years
- 3 years
- 4 years
- 5 years

## Files

- `index.html` - Main HTML structure
- `styles.css` - Styling and responsive design
- `script.js` - Milestone data and application logic

## Example Usage

**Example 1**: Enter "18" and select "months" to see milestones for an 18-month-old child.

**Example 2**: Enter "3" and select "years" to see milestones for a 3-year-old child.

## Important Disclaimer

This tool provides general developmental milestones based on typical child development patterns. Every child develops at their own unique pace. If you have concerns about your child's development, please consult with a healthcare professional or pediatrician.

## Data Source

Milestone data is based on guidelines from pediatric developmental standards, including recommendations from the CDC and American Academy of Pediatrics.
